# Product Guidelines — OpenCourseDeck

## Brand & Aesthetic Principles
- **Tone**: Focused, Tactile, Precision-Crafted, Ergonomic, Luminous.
- **Visual Theme**: Deep Obsidian + Solar Emerald + Cyber Cyan (OKLCH color system).
- **Typography**: Inter (Display/Body) + JetBrains Mono (Telemetry/Tabular) + Playfair Display (Serif Reading Mode).

## Design & UX Standards
- **Zero-Slop Rule**: Avoid generic purple/indigo gradients,Inter-only monoculture, and unstyled component resting states.
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)**: Explicit focus-visible rings, keyboard operability, minimum 24px interactive touch targets.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` across all transitions and animations.
